## Balance notes

- **Early-game:**
  - **La Comparsa** is strong early (+16 Mult with 4 empty slots) and fades as the troupe
    fills: a good opener to sell later.
  - **Burattino** goes the other way: it grows as you add Jesters.
- **Big hands:** Rosaura and Florindo make Full House and Four of a Kind builds possible
  from the first shops, before any rare turns up.
- **Money:**
  - **Mirandolina:** steady +3.
  - **Il Bigliettaio:** a savings box you sell later.
  - **Il Trovarobe:** rewards holding consumables instead of using them, which
    competes with Il Cantastorie.
- **Clubs:** Tabarrino (uncommon) grows only when Clubs score. Il Mondo, the Tarocchi
  that turns cards into Clubs, makes it a real build.
